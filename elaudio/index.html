01 / What it does

Three ways to
not sound like you.

LIVE VOICES

Old lady. Middle-aged man. Your own.

Talk and hear the new voice straight away. The two starting voices are tuned against real recordings, and you can tweak them or build your own.

  • Pitch, formants and auto pitch
  • Tremor, vibrato and gravel
  • EQ, room and a safety limiter
VOICE CLONING

Clone a voice on your Mac.

Read a few sentences from the screen for 15 to 20 seconds and you have a clone. Add more samples later to make it sound closer.

  • Use a clone as a live filter
  • Or type text and hear it spoken
  • Keep as many voices as you like
RECORD & EXPORT

Clips, not just calls.

Record a clip in your normal voice and play it back in any voice. Open audio files you already have, and export the result to share.

  • Choose the input and output device
  • Hear yourself on headphones
  • Keyboard shortcuts for Live, Record and Play
02 / In calls

Your call app hears
the new voice.

  1. Install the virtual mic

    ELAudio comes with its own virtual microphone, ELAudio Mic. Installing it takes one click and your password, once.

  2. Turn on “Use in calls”

    Pick a voice and press Talk Live. ELAudio becomes your Mac’s microphone, so WhatsApp, FaceTime and Zoom use it automatically.

  3. Stop, and you’re you again

    Your real microphone comes back as soon as you stop. Natural voice lets you speak normally without hanging up.

In Discord, choose ELAudio Mic and turn off noise suppression. Wear headphones so there’s no echo.

Privacy

Your voice stays on your Mac.

Voice changing and voice cloning run on this Mac. Recordings and cloned voices are saved only on your computer. No account, nothing uploaded. The cloning models download once, the first time you set up cloning.

Use it kindly

Only clone voices you’re allowed to.

Clone your own voice, or the voice of someone who said yes. Don’t use a cloned voice to trick people, get money or get into accounts. Pranks are funnier when everyone’s laughing at the end.

03 / Questions

Good to know.

What do I need?

A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) on macOS 14 Sonoma or later. The live voices work straight away. Voice cloning also needs git, uv and ffmpeg, which you can install with Homebrew: brew install git uv ffmpeg. ELAudio then sets up the open-source cloning lab and downloads its models, which are a few gigabytes.

macOS says it can’t check the app. What do I do?

ELAudio isn’t notarized by Apple yet. Open it once, then go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. macOS will also ask for microphone permission. Only open apps you trust; you can compare the SHA-256 below with your download.

How fast is a cloned voice live?

Expect about half a second of delay, depending on your Mac. The Delay setting lets you trade a bit more delay for a clearer voice. The built-in voices (Old Lady, Middle-Aged Man and your own) are practically instant.

Does the virtual mic change anything else on my Mac?

Only while Talk Live runs with “Use in calls” on: it becomes the default microphone, and your previous mic is put back when you stop. You can remove it at any time from the app (How to set up… › Remove Virtual Mic).

Why do I hear an echo?

If you’re on speakers, the new voice can leak back into the mic. Use headphones. ELAudio already turns off “Hear myself” on speakers for this reason.
